我对很多平台跳跃游戏是又爱又恨。

I have a love-hate relationship with platformers.


爱在它们可以兼容各种不同有趣的机制。


I love them because they can incorporate all kinds of interesting mechanics.


恨在我的左手太酸了。

I hate them because my left hand gets tired.


我想做一个比较反常规的平台跳跃游戏。

So I wanted to make a slightly unconventional platformer.


不会再受限于移动的规则，而是想在空中跳几次就跳几次，让你们自己安排。


One that isn’t restricted by traditional movement rules. If you want to jump multiple times in the air, you can. It’s up to you.


并且，我想让你们的右手也动起来！


And I also want to get your right hand involved.
