你应该玩过，或者听说过GTA V。


You’ve probably played, or at least heard of, GTA V.


一个很自由的游戏，对吧？


It’s a very open game, right?


你可以烧杀抢夺，纯凭你的心意行事，因为你是游戏里的主角。


You can do whatever you want, burn, steal, kill, because you’re the main character.


但是，我们好像从来没在乎过，那个在街上走着的，无法被交互的，被你随意射杀，倒在地上的血泊里，没有名字的她/他的故事。


But we rarely think about the people walking down the street:
the ones you can’t really interact with,
the ones you can shoot without a second thought,
the ones who fall to the ground, nameless, forgotten.


这个游戏是献给她们的，那些被忽视的人。


This game is for them.
For the ones who are overlooked.


这个游戏还处于非常早期的阶段。我们会持续更新它，把它做到完整，为了他们。


This game is still in a very early stage.
We’ll keep working on it and continue developing it into something more complete, especially for them.
