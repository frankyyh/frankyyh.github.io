这个游戏的主题是迷宫。


The theme of this game is a maze.


但我又不想做一个那种，普通的，很常见的迷宫。


But I didn’t want to make a typical, conventional maze.


我更想做一种类似于门和钥匙的感觉。


I was more interested in something closer to a “door and key” dynamic.


门就在这里，但是你得先找到钥匙才能通过。


The door is right there, but you need to find the key before you can pass through.


于是这个游戏就诞生了。


That’s how this game came into being.


另外一个我在尝试的东西是感官的探索。
听觉、视觉、触觉，移动的方向，以及最重要的，如何营造那种在2D俯视角的角度上跳跃的感觉？


Another thing I’ve been exploring is perception:
sound, visuals, touch, direction of movement, and most importantly, how to create the feeling of jumping within a 2D top-down perspective.
